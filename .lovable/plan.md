# Refine Credentials and Process Sections

## Goal
Make the homepage Recognition & Credentials and Process sections feel substantially more premium, readable, and intentional on both mobile and laptop while preserving the existing brand, copy, links, and order.

## Changes
- Recompose Recognition & Credentials as a strong editorial proof block with a numbered credential list, clearer institution/date hierarchy, and a more prominent certificates link.
- Recompose Process as a sequential four-step journey with stronger numbering, clearer visual flow, and better text rhythm.
- Add only section-specific responsive styling so these improvements do not alter unrelated pages or content.
- Preserve the cream, muted sage, and walnut palette and existing motion system.

## Responsive treatment
- Mobile: single-column flow, comfortable tap target, compact spacing, no clipped text or horizontal overflow.
- Laptop/desktop: balanced two-part layouts, denser vertical rhythm, aligned columns, and controlled line lengths.

## Validation
Check the homepage at mobile, 1366×768, 1440×900, and 1920×1080 for visual balance, wrapping, overflow, and working navigation.
